⚠️ Important Training Notes

  • Warm-up: Always spend 5-10 minutes warming up before training
  • Form First: Quality over quantity - maintain proper form throughout
  • Progression: Only increase difficulty when you can complete all sets with good form
  • Recovery: Rest days are crucial for muscle growth and injury prevention
  • Nutrition: Fuel your body with adequate protein and calories for optimal results
  • Hydration: Stay well-hydrated before, during, and after workouts
  • Listen to Your Body: Stop if you feel pain (beyond normal muscle fatigue)
